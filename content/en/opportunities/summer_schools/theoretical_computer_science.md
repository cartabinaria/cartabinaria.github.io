Some of the following websites only feature edition-specific pages. When this
happens, a link to the most recent edition at the time of writing will be
present.

## [_Advanced Functional Programming Summer School_ (AFP)](https://uu-afp.github.io/)

This Dutch summer school aims to educate aspiring Haskell programmers beyond the
basic material covered by many textbooks.

## [_Dutch Winter School on Logic and Verification_](https://cyclic-structures.gitlab.io/school2026)

This Dutch school covers topic in theoretical computer science, especially
program verification, logic, and type theory.

## [_European Summer School in Logic, Language and Information_ (ESSLLI)](https://2026.esslli.eu/)

Founded in 1989, this european summer school is perhaps the most popular logic
summer school in Europe.

## [_International School on Rewriting_ (ISR)](https://isr2026.cs.ru.nl/)

The _International School on Rewriting_ (ISR) focuses on term rewriting.
riscrittura dei termini.

## [Midlands Graduate School in the Foundations of Computing Science (MGS)](https://www.cs.nott.ac.uk/MGS/)

First held in 1999, this English school is focused on the mathematical
foundations of computing. It is held in spring, rather than in summer or in
winter.

## [_Oregon Programming Languages Summer School_ (OPLSS)](https://www.cs.uoregon.edu/research/summerschool)

This US school is about programming languages and their theoretical foundations.

## [_Scottish Programming Languages and Verification Summer School_ (SPLV)](https://spli.scot/splv/)

This popular Scottish school ranges from theoretical to applied topics, all
concerning programming languages and formal verification.
